NOTES — 2026-08-26
==================

Four things to pick up tomorrow. Your notes are verbatim; the indented
lines under each are just pointers to where the work would land, so you
don't have to go looking.


1. "add new choices, like signing to a label, or staying on tour with
    an artist"

    Cards live in src/content/cards.js. There are 48 right now:
    21 green (regular), 20 yellow (gamble), 7 repeatable filler.
    The schema is documented at the top of that file.

    Both examples want something the deck cannot express yet: a choice
    whose consequences run for SEVERAL YEARS rather than resolving in
    the one you took it. Signing to a label and staying out on tour are
    both multi-year commitments, and right now every card starts and
    finishes inside a single turn.

    So this is probably a small engine feature first — a commitment
    that occupies the next N years, or gates/forces what gets drawn
    while it runs — and then cards written against it. The existing
    'manager_pending' flag is the closest thing in there already: it is
    set at signing and revealed years later, so it is a decent model to
    copy.


2. "genre specific choices"

    Every roster artist already carries a `genre` field
    (src/content/roster.js), and roster.js also has GENRE_TO_CHART
    mapping 94 genre tags onto the 12 real Billboard chart categories.
    None of it is read by the card system today.

    Worth deciding up front which of these you mean, because they pull
    in different directions:
      - cards that only appear if you have worked in a genre
      - a genre identity you accumulate and that then shapes your draws
      - genre as something you can deliberately switch, at a cost

    The third is the most interesting and the most work.


3. "fix site scaling to fit in the same screen without having to
    scroll"

    src/styles.css. The Career Review screen is a two-column grid
    (.screen-l / .screen-r) with a 6-row log; the log folding is what
    keeps the height bounded, controlled by LOG_ROWS in src/App.jsx.

    Known departure from the original design handoff, written in the
    header comment of styles.css: the mock was a fixed 1920x1080 frame
    scaled to fit, and this is fluid instead. Getting to no-scroll
    probably means going back toward the mock — a fixed aspect frame
    that scales — or capping the whole thing at 100vh and letting the
    log take whatever is left.

    Check the end screen and the trophy cabinet too, not just the turn
    screen. The cabinet is the longest page in the game.


4. "fix text clutterness, too much info on the screen"

    Related to 3 and probably worth doing first — if less is on screen,
    the scaling problem gets smaller on its own.

    Densest areas, roughly in order:
      - the year log: year / age / action / plaques / awards / outcome
        is six columns
      - the ON TAPE strip, which repeats credit information that is
        also in the log
      - the OVR card: number, delta, "since YEAR", the decile bar and
        the talent line, all stacked
      - option cards carry title, body, and now the odds line on yellow
        ones
